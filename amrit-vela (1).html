‹ Scoring & Analytics
🪔 Panj Granthi Bani Points
Each Bani's individual share of the combined Panj Granthi weight. These don't need to add up to any particular total — your Panj Granthi score is always (points of Banis done ÷ points of all five), so any ratio works. Changing these recalculates your entire history, same as any other weight change.
